How I work

Assume nothing

Experts forget what beginners don't know. Every guide I make starts from zero: no jargon without a definition, no step skipped because it's "obvious."

Show, don't lecture

Nobody learns a new system from a slide about it. I use live builds and real workflows, so people leave with something working, not something conceptual.

Build for the day after the demo

Adoption dies when the demo ends. I pair every session with repeatable materials, templates, and a home where knowledge accumulates instead of evaporating.

About

I'm a product manager with nine years in enterprise software: five implementing enterprise systems for large clients as a consultant and solution architect, and four running an internal developer platform used by more than 1,500 engineers every month.

That combination is the point. I've been the person explaining complex systems to skeptical rooms, and the person responsible for whether a platform actually gets adopted. Adoption is the same problem no matter the tool: the technology is rarely the bottleneck. The teaching is.
